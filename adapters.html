Tested Adapters

Spotter needs an adapter that handles header switching and multi-frame replies reliably at speed. These are the ones we've verified on a real Land Cruiser 250.

AdapterStatusNotes
OBDLink CXVerified The development reference. Bluetooth LE, STN2310. Requires one-time pairing on first connect — accept the iOS pairing prompt.
OBDLink MX+Expected to work Same STN family as the CX; not yet verified on this vehicle.
Generic BLE "ELM327 v1.5" clonesNot recommended Many drop multi-frame replies or mangle fast polling. Spotter runs a capability check on connect and will tell you if your adapter fails it, rather than failing silently.
Any Wi-Fi adapterUnsupported Joining an adapter's Wi-Fi network breaks wireless CarPlay. By design, never supported.

Tested an adapter we haven't? Tell us — model, firmware string if the app shows one, and what worked or didn't.
